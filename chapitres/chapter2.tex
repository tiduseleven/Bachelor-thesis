\chapter{Expanding to $n$-exact categories}\label{chapter:nexact}
After generalizing from abelian categories to exact categories, we can go one step further by looking at longer exact sequences.

\section{Working in higher degrees}
Before defining $n$-exact categories, there are many prerequisites that need to be addressed. Firstly, we will need a way to properly describe what the equivalent of a kernel-cokernel pair would be in that new context. As a result, it makes sense to define $n$-kernels and $n$-cokernels, which require the notion of weak kernel (respectively weak cokernel).
